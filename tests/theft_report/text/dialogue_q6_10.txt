OFFICER: Right, let’s come to the theft itself. What date did it happen?
DANIEL: The 30th of June.
OFFICER: The 13th of June?
DANIEL: No, the 30th — three-zero June.
OFFICER: Where had you been that evening, and where did you last definitely have the laptop?
DANIEL: I used it in a café for about half an hour, then put it back in my bag and went to the cinema. After the film, I sat on a low wall outside the cinema to check the bus times and placed the laptop beside me. I’m certain it was still there then.
OFFICER: When did you realise it had gone, and approximately what time was that?
DANIEL: At the bus stop, less than five minutes later. I went straight back, but it wasn’t on the wall. The cinema staff checked the foyer and lost property, and I also returned to the café, although I knew I’d had it after leaving there. It was around eight o’clock; the film had finished at about quarter to eight.
OFFICER: Did anyone approach you while you were sitting outside?
DANIEL: Yes. A young man came over and asked me for the time. I looked down at my phone to answer, so for a few seconds I wasn’t watching the laptop. He then walked away towards the car park.
OFFICER: Can you describe him?
DANIEL: He was about eighteen, tall and slim, with blond hair. He had a dark jacket, blue jeans and white trainers, and he was carrying a large black sports bag over one shoulder. I didn’t notice a logo or any other distinctive mark.
OFFICER: Was anyone else close enough to have seen what happened?
DANIEL: Several people were coming out of the cinema, but nobody was standing beside us. A woman was waiting near the entrance, though, and she may have seen him leave.
OFFICER: All right. I’ll ask the cinema whether they have security-camera footage. Your crime reference number is PT6048. Keep it somewhere safe because your insurer will ask for it, and use it if you send us the serial number or any further information.
DANIEL: I will. I’ll email the receipt and serial number tomorrow morning. Thanks for your help.
OFFICER: You’re welcome. We’ll be in touch if we need anything else.
